Voltworks Guide

Power, cables and machines, from your first generator to a nuclear reactor.

---
Power

Generator: burns furnace fuel, 10 J/t. One coal = 4,000 J.

Solar Panel: 1 J/t in daylight under open sky.

Thermal Generator: 5 J/t per lava source touching it.
---
Cables & Batteries

Copper Cable carries power between blocks.

Power goes to machines first, then to batteries. The front gauge shows the charge.
---
Batteries

Small: 25,000 J
Medium: 100,000 J
Large: 1,000,000 J
Huge: 10,000,000 J

Bigger batteries also charge and discharge faster.
---
Machines

Crusher: ore into 2 dust.
Electric Furnace: smelts with power.
Alloy Smelter: iron + 2 coal into steel.

Steel makes Circuits and the Advanced Machine Frame.
---
Tier 2

Industrial Crusher: 3 dust per ore, 4x speed.

Induction Furnace: two lanes, 4x speed.

Upgrades (3 slots): Speed, Capacity, Ejector.
---
Item Pipes

Glass pipes join chests, hoppers and machines. You can watch items travel.

Machine to chest just works: pipes pull finished items out of machines into chests.
---
Item Pipes

To pull from a chest, sneak + right-click that pipe arm with the wrench: Extract (striped collar).

Right-click an arm with the wrench to connect or disconnect it.
---
Pipe Tiers

Item Pipe: 4 items/s.
Fast (red): 16 items/s.
Express (gold): 64 items/s, near instant.

The pipe touching the source sets the rate.
---
Filter Pipe

Right-click it to set up to 9 items.

Allow: only those pass.
Deny: those are blocked.

Put one in front of a chest to sort into it.
---
Wrench

Right-click the air to switch Rotate / Configure.

Rotate: turn machines to face the side you click.

Sneak + right-click to pick up a Voltworks block.
---
Side Config

In Configure mode, right-click a machine side to cycle it:

None, Input, Output, Both.

Settings follow the machine when you rotate it.
---
Tools

Electric Drill, Chainsaw and Advanced Drill run on energy.

Recharge them in the Charger.

Advanced Drill: sneak + right-click for 3x3 mining.
---
Chainsaw

Cut one log and the whole tree comes down, as long as it has natural leaves.

Each log costs power as usual.

Sneak to cut a single log.
---
Uranium

Uranium can't be smelted or crushed.

The Uranium Refiner enriches Raw Uranium into Refined Uranium. It needs lots of power.

Fuel Rods are made from Refined Uranium.
---
Reprocessing

Put a spent Fuel Rod in the Uranium Refiner to get one Refined Uranium back.

Or crush it to recover its lead.
---
Reactor

3x3 grid of Fuel Rods and Heat Vents. Needs redstone. Pauses when its buffer is full.

Rods side by side make more power and much more heat.

Keep heat below 100% or it melts down! A comparator reads heat.
---
Computer Storage

Smelt 2 sand + coal in the Alloy Smelter for Silicon, then craft Silicon Chips.

Hard Drives hold items: 1K, 4K, 16K or 64K, up to 63 kinds each.
---
Drive Rack

Holds 8 Hard Drives. Needs power: 1 J/t plus 1 per drive.

Link racks, terminals and crafters with Data Cable (or let them touch).
---
Storage Terminal

See and search everything stored.

Click: take a stack.
Right-click: half.
Shift-click: to inventory.

Click holding an item to store it.
---
Computer Crafter

Click list items to fill the 3x3 pattern, then press x1, x8 or x64.

Ingredients come from storage; results go back into it.

More space: add drives or racks.
