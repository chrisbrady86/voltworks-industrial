Getting Started
@items generator copper_cable crusher electric_furnace wrench
Place a Generator, feed it coal and run Copper Cable to a machine.

Hover over any Voltworks item to see what it does. With JEI installed, every recipe and machine is listed there.
---
Power
@image power
@items generator solar_panel thermal_generator copper_cable battery_box
Generators burn fuel, Solar Panels need daylight, Thermal Generators sit next to lava.

Machines get power first; batteries store the rest. Bigger batteries hold more and charge faster.
---
Machines
@image machines
@items crusher electric_furnace alloy_smelter industrial_crusher induction_furnace
Crusher: one ore makes two dust, so two ingots.

Alloy Smelter: Steel and Silicon.

Industrial Crusher and Induction Furnace: faster tier 2.
---
Upgrades
@items speed_upgrade capacity_upgrade ejector_upgrade
Every machine has 3 upgrade slots.

Speed: works faster, uses more power.
Capacity: stores more power.
Ejector: pushes finished items into the next machine or chest.
---
Item Pipes
@image pipes
@items item_pipe fast_item_pipe express_item_pipe filter_pipe wrench
Pipes empty machines into chests by themselves.

To pull from a chest, sneak + right-click the pipe arm with the Wrench. Faster pipes: red, then gold.
---
Filter Pipe
@items filter_pipe
Right-click it to choose up to 9 items.

Allow: only those pass.
Deny: those are blocked.

Put one in front of a chest to sort items into it.
---
Wrench
@items wrench
Right-click the air to switch between Rotate and Configure.

Configure: click a machine side to set None, Input, Output or Both.

Click a pipe arm to connect or disconnect it.

Sneak + right-click picks up any Voltworks block.
---
Power Tools
@items electric_drill chainsaw advanced_drill charger
Tools run on energy and never break. Recharge them in the Charger.

Chainsaw: fells the whole tree. Sneak to cut one log.

Advanced Drill: sneak + right-click for 3x3 mining.
---
Chunk Loader
@items chunk_loader
Chunks only run while a player is near. A Chunk Loader keeps the 3x3 chunks around it running while you're away, so machines, quarries and reactors keep working.

Right-click it to see which chunks. A redstone signal switches it off.
---
Ores
@items tin_ore lead_ore uranium_ore raw_tin raw_lead raw_uranium
Tin is found at mid depth, Lead deep down and Uranium deepest of all.

Uranium can't be smelted or crushed: enrich it in the Uranium Refiner.
---
Uranium
@items uranium_refiner refined_uranium fuel_rod depleted_fuel_rod
The Uranium Refiner turns Raw Uranium into Refined Uranium for Fuel Rods.

Spent rods go back into the Refiner for more uranium, or into a Crusher for their lead.
---
Reactor
@image reactor
@items reactor_chamber fuel_rod heat_vent lead_block
Fill the grid with Fuel Rods and Heat Vents and give it redstone. Rods side by side: more power, far more heat.

At 100% heat it melts down! Two layers of Lead Blocks contain the blast.
---
Computer Storage
@image storage
@items drive_rack small_hard_drive data_cable storage_terminal computer_crafter
Put Hard Drives in a powered Drive Rack and link it with Data Cable.

Storage Terminal: search, take and store. Computer Crafter: crafts straight from storage.
---
Storage Interface
@items storage_interface filter_pipe item_pipe
Lets item pipes reach your storage.

A plain arm puts items in. An Extract arm pulls them out; add a Filter Pipe to choose which.

Example: Interface, Filter (Raw Tin), Crusher, and a pipe back for the dust.
